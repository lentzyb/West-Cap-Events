Monday recruiting analytics update
- Reads both Orientation and Active Roster groups.
- Combines and deduplicates Monday items by item ID before matching.
- A matched Monday candidate is permanently stored using the Monday item ID, not the registration ID.
- Multiple event registrations for the same Monday candidate do not create multiple recruits.
- Moving a candidate from Orientation to Active Roster does not increase the recruit count.
- Historical saved recruit attribution remains after the candidate later leaves either group.
- Existing older saved attributions are deduplicated during reporting by Monday item ID when available.
